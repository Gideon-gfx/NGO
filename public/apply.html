Apply To Lagos Sistema

Free instruments, free lessons, and a place in an ensemble – for children ages 3–16 from underserved Lagos communities.

Who Can Apply

Open To Children Ages 6–16

Lagos Sistema is completely free for families from our partner communities. No prior musical experience is needed – just a willingness to show up, practice, and play as part of a group. Spaces are limited and allocated by hub.

Submit Your Application

Fill out the form below with your child's details and preferred hub.

Meet Your Hub

Our team will contact you to schedule a short in-person visit to your chosen hub.

Instrument Assignment

Your child is placed into the right track for their age and assigned an instrument to keep.

Start Lessons

Weekly group lessons and rehearsals begin – free, for as long as your child wants to play.

Tell Us About Your Child
