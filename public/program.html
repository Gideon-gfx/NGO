Program

Free Instruments, Free Instruction

A structured, age-based pathway from a child's very first note to full orchestra – every step of it free.

Overview

A Track For Every Age

Students move through five stages as they grow, each building on the skills, confidence and repertoire of the last. Every stage is taught in ensemble – students learn to play with each other, not just for themselves.

Ages 6–8

Young Voices

Choir-based foundations in pitch, breath and group singing, preparing students for their first instrument.

Ages 8–10

String & Wind Starters

First instruments are assigned and kept – strings, winds and percussion – with weekly group lessons.

Ages 10–13

Ensemble Players

Students join a full rehearsing ensemble, tackling real repertoire and their first public performances.

Ages 13–16

Lagos Sistema Youth Orchestra

Our senior ensemble – advanced repertoire, leadership roles, and a pathway into teaching and mentorship.

Teachers

Trained, Local Teaching Artists

Our Teaching Artists are Lagos-based musicians trained in ensemble pedagogy and youth mentorship. Many are graduates of Nigerian conservatories and university music programs; several are Lagos Sistema alumni themselves.
