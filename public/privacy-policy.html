Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Lagos Sistema respects the privacy and dignity of the children, families, staff and supporters represented in our community gallery.

Gallery verification

If you request gallery access, we use your Gmail address to send a one-time verification code and, after successful verification, a gallery thank-you message. The code expires after 10 minutes. Gallery access is temporary and must be verified again on a later visit. Your address is not added to our mailing list by requesting a gallery code.

Mailing list

We use an email address for updates only when you separately choose to subscribe and agree to receive them.

Gallery media

Gallery photos and videos are stored encrypted on our server and made available to verified visitors for viewing. Please respect the people shown and do not copy, record or share media without permission. Browser display cannot prevent screenshots or other forms of capture.

Data handling

Verification codes are stored as protected values and expire. Access tokens are temporary. We use request limits to help prevent misuse. We do not sell gallery verification email addresses. Email delivery is handled through the Gmail account configured for Lagos Sistema.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact lagossistema@gmail.com.
