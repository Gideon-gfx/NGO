About

Empowering Kids Through Music

Our mission is to empower children and strengthen community through the shared language of music.

We do this by giving children from underserved Lagos communities free instruments, along with free group and individual music lessons. Playing and singing together builds confidence, discipline, teamwork and leadership that carries far beyond the rehearsal room.

Programs

Free Instruments, Free Instruction

Lagos Sistema's programs serve low-income communities and most kids participate for free. Removing cost as a barrier lets our children try something they couldn't have imagined before – from the recorder to the trumpet.
