Slum2School Partnership

Lagos Sistema has partnered with Slum2School to lead and manage its music education pillar at their Makoko hub (Adekunle Anglican Primary School). Through the new music studio lab, we have launched an intensive 4-week music camp (August 6September 2026) for 50 youths.

This inaugural program paves the way for our upcoming educational initiatives and will conclude with a live student showcase on September 19th. Students receive dedicated instruction in:

  • Strings: Violin, Viola, Cello, Double Bass
  • Winds: Flute, Recorder, Clarinet, Saxophone, Trumpet, Trombone
  • Percussion: Glockenspiel, Drum Kit
  • Chords: Piano, Guitar
  • Voice: Singing

Help Us Keep the Music Playing Across Lagos and its Suburbs

We are actively seeking instrument donations and student sponsorships to support more youths across the city of Lagos and its suburban regions as we hope to create more partnerships and hubs to revitalize and reshape the future of music across all Lagos communities.
